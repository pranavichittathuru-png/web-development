🌸"Krishna"🌸

🌼Lord Krishna is my favourite god because his life and teachings offer a perfect blend of profound wisdom, divine love, and relatable humanity.
Unlike a distant deity, Krishna is celebrated in many roles—as a mischievous child stealing butter, a loyal friend, a passionate lover, and a righteous warrior. As the narrator of the Bhagavad Gita, he delivers timeless spiritual guidance, teaching us how to navigate life's duties with detachment and devotion.

🥗🤍His favourite foods

  • Makhan (Butter)
  • Mishri (Rock Sugar)
  • Poha / Gopalkala
  • Panjiri & Dhaniya Panjiri
  • Kheer
  • Peda
  • Panchamrit
  • Fresh Seasonal Fruits

Abode Goloka, Vrindavan, Gokul, Mathura, Dvaraka
Birthplace Mathura, Uttar Pradesh, India
Parents Vasudeva and Devaki (foster parents: Nanda and Yashoda)
Major Role Guiding deity and charioteer in the Kurukshetra War (Mahabharata)
