My feeling about this movie:

The beautiful movie Shyam Singha Roy is a visually stunning and emotionally powerful film that beautifully balances commercial grandeur with artistic depth. Directed by Rahul Sankrityan, the movie shines brightest during its period portions, making it an incredibly satisfying watch despite a few pacing flaws in the modern timeline.
