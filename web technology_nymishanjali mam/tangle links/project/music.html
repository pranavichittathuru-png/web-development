My Music Playlist

Music is a quiet escape that brings true peace to the mind and fills the soul with a comforting melody. Every gentle note feels like a safe place to rest during loud and busy days. Among all the wonderful tunes in the world, "Perfect" by Ed Sheeran holds a very special place in my heart. With its sweet acoustic guitar and warm, romantic story, it captures the purest feeling of love and happiness. Listening to it always makes the world feel quiet, beautiful, and completely right

My Favourite Artist

Lana Del Rey is my favorite musical artist, known for her dreamy voice and nostalgic, cinematic style that makes me feel like i am living in an old movie. Her famous song "Brooklyn Baby" is a cool and moody track about city life, art, and having a musician boyfriend, capturing her unique and vintage vibe in a truly special way.
